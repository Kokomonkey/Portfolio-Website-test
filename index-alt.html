About

Douwe

I'm an architecture student working out how AI belongs in the everyday architectural process — as co-creation rather than automation.

This site is two things at once: the work itself, and a transparent record of the methods behind it. Where a drawing was generated, post-processed or parametrically driven, I say so and show how.

It's an ongoing experiment. If something is missing, wrong, or could be built better, I'd rather hear it.
