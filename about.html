About the Hub

Who Am I

I am an architecture student exploring how to implement AI into the everyday architectural process. My work focuses on how algorithms can augment the productive and creative process, moving beyond simple automation to genuine co-creation.

Locations of study:

  • - TU Delft 2024 - current [Architecture Bachelor]
  • - NUS Singapore 2025 [Non-faculty based Exchange] (Focused on architecture)

This is my first proper website. It is meant to both display my capabilities and serve as a source of information for other architects and students.

What is this Webpage

This hub serves as a living documentation of my AI knowledge. It is not just a portfolio, but a deconstruction of the workflow I use to generate architectural concepts, providing insight into what is possible.

Here, I break down the specific AIs available, share starter tips and tricks, and explain the post-processing techniques used to achieve the best results. It is designed to be transparent.
