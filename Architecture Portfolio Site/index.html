Convertible Convergence

NUS Singapore · Design 5 · 2025
FIG. 01 — INTERIOR PERSPECTIVE / N–E
Site Singapore
Studio NUS DES5
Year 2025
Programme Adaptive Civic
Software Rhino · GH · Enscape
Output Plans · Renders · Poster

An architectural answer to urban edges that refuse to settle.

Convertible Convergence proposes a building whose interior is not designed once, but reconfigured continuously. Floors host sliding bookcases, foldable tables and bendable furniture systems — each calibrated to a different rhythm of use throughout the day.

The building sits where conflicting urban plans collide. Rather than picking a side, the project metabolises both: a dynamic façade tunes its envelope to context, while the structural grid lets the floor plate read as workshop, library, gathering space or theatre depending on the hour. A single building, many programmes.

About — Douwe
