TU Delft M.Arch · 2-year plan

Ordered roadmap — study, portfolio, book, game, internship

One sequence for the next two years of your M.Arch, tuned to your reality: Rhino-strong but light on code, aiming at a big, creative office like MVRDV, holding studio to ≤ 40 h/week so you keep the space to stand out. The book and game both begin now with a two-month concept sprint and must reach a first draft and a playable demo by graduation. Numbered projects run roughly in order, front-loaded so the low-effort tools pay off all masters long.

Ordered projects — what comes first, what comes next

Since you're light on code, build these no-code first: custom GPTs (Brief Decoder, Concept Writer, Crit Simulator), NotebookLM / Obsidian (Knowledge Garden), ComfyUI (Drawing Style Transfer, Model→Render), CapCut (Reel Machine). Lean on Grasshopper — your real strength — for the CAD-side tools. The one that genuinely wants a developer is Detail Doctor; pull in one of your game-dev friends for it.
